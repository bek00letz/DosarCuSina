## Description

Dosar cu Șină is the household binder for the paperwork of Romanian life: the car's periodic inspection (ITP) and insurance (RCA), the road vignette, the boiler check, passports and identity cards, a pet's vaccines, warranties and utility bills. These come round rarely but predictably, and they catch people unprepared. A "dosar cu șină" is the slotted folder Romanian households keep their papers in; the app is that folder on the iPhone and iPad.

Capture comes first. A photo of a document, or a PDF shared from Mail or WhatsApp, is read on the device, filed in the right place, and turned into a deadline with reminders that come as early as each renewal needs. The binder opens on one box per thing the household looks after, each with one line that says whether all is well, and the list of what's next stays short.

Built on Apple's frameworks: the on-device language model and Vision read documents without sending them anywhere; widgets close a deadline with one tap; Siri and Shortcuts say what is due and mark it done; Spotlight finds what is in the binder; Face ID guards the categories a person chooses; and the binder syncs through the person's own iCloud.

It's free, with no account, no ads, no in-app purchases and no data collected.

## Helpful details

- **Accessibility and plain language.** Made for people who aren't comfortable with technology as much as for those who are: four labelled tabs, large tap targets, Dynamic Type up to the accessibility sizes, VoiceOver labels throughout, Reduce Motion respected, and copy written in plain Romanian with no jargon.
- **Privacy.** "Data Not Collected" in App Privacy. Documents are read on the device; the binder stays on the device and in the person's private iCloud database. An optional AI provider is used only with the person's own key, for a document they choose, after they agree.
- **Local relevance.** It knows Romanian documents and rules: identity cards and passports, the car's registration certificate, RCA and CASCO policies, the PAD home insurance, utility bills and their meter readings, and which documents a trip abroad needs.
- **The team.** An independent developer in Romania. The source is public on GitHub.
