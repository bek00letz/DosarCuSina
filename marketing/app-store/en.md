# App Store: the English listing (optional)

**Optional, the owner's call.** The app speaks Romanian only. An English listing reaches people browsing the App Store in English (Romanians abroad, people in Romania whose phone is set to English) and has to say plainly that the app is in Romanian. Without it, App Store shows the Romanian listing everywhere. If it ships, it is added in App Store Connect as an English (U.K. or U.S.) localization, with the same screenshots (they show the app as it is, in Romanian). The words match [site/en](../../site/en/index.html); the counts are checked by `marketing/check-listing.py`.

## Description (4000)

```text
The household’s paper binder, on your phone: documents, deadlines and bills, with reminders in good time. The app is in Romanian, made for Romanian paperwork.

The car’s ITP inspection and RCA insurance, the passport, the boiler check, the PAD home policy, the cat’s vaccines, the washing machine’s warranty. They all have a date, and they all come when you least expect them. Put each one in the binder once, and it reminds you as early as the renewal needs, not earlier.

TAKE A PHOTO, IT READS IT
Photograph the registration certificate, the policy or the bill, or send it from Mail or WhatsApp with Share. The phone reads the dates, amounts and numbers and shows you where it goes before anything is saved. PDFs work too.

WHAT’S NEXT, AND ONLY THAT
A short list of what comes next, however much is in the binder. When something is done, tap “Am rezolvat” and the next date follows: the inspection from the new report, the policy from the new policy.

A BOX FOR EACH THING
The car, the home, the household’s people and their documents, pets, warranties, subscriptions. Each has its own page: deadlines, documents, services and repairs, what was spent and who did the work.

GOING ABROAD?
A checklist of the documents each traveller and the car need: an identity card or a passport, how long each is still valid, what is missing. The same when selling the car or making a claim.

YOURS, ON YOUR PHONE
No account. No ads, no tracking. It works without the internet, and what you put in the binder stays on your phone. Documents and health can sit behind Face ID. Make a password-protected backup whenever you like.

AND MORE
• Search the whole binder, from the iPhone’s search too.
• Widgets on the Home Screen and the Lock Screen.
• Siri and Shortcuts.
• Odometer, meter readings and bills, with their history.
• The people and firms who look after the home, with their phone numbers at hand.

Optionally, add your own key for an AI service (Google Gemini, OpenAI, Claude or a compatible one) to read difficult documents better. Without it, everything works the same: reading on the phone, reminders, widgets, backups.

Free, with no subscription and nothing to buy in the app. The app’s code is public on GitHub.
```
