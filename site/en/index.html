Dosar cu Șină

The household’s paper binder, on the phone: documents, deadlines and bills, with reminders in good time. The car’s inspection and insurance, the passport, the boiler check, the cat’s vaccine, the washing machine’s warranty.

It’s free, with no account and no ads. It works without the internet, and what you put in it stays on your phone. The app is in Romanian.
