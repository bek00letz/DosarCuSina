Help

Dosar cu Șină is the household’s paper binder, on the phone: documents, deadlines and bills, with reminders in good time. It’s free, and works without an account and without the internet. The app is in Romanian.

Common questions

  • Where is my data?

    On your phone and, when iCloud sync is on, in your own iCloud. No one else sees it. More in the privacy policy.

  • How do I move it to a new phone?

    On the old phone, in Setări › iCloud și copii de siguranță, tap “Fă o copie de siguranță” (make a backup) and send yourself the file. On the new phone, in the same place, tap “Pune înapoi o copie” (restore a backup) and choose the file. With iCloud sync, the binder comes to the new phone by itself, with the same account.

  • I forgot my backup’s password.

    It can’t be recovered: neither the app nor anyone else knows it. If you still have the old phone, make a new backup from it.

  • I don’t get reminders.

    Look in the app’s Setări › Notificări. If it says “Oprite din Setările telefonului” (off in the phone’s settings), turn them on in the iPhone’s Settings, under Notifications › Dosar cu Șină.

  • Do I need an AI key?

    No. Everything works without one. With a key of your own, documents that are hard to read are read better. Add one, if you want, in Setări › Citire cu AI.

  • Face ID doesn’t recognise me.

    You can always use the phone’s passcode. If you cancel, the locked part stays closed, and opens when you try again.

Get help

Write your question on the app’s questions and problems page, on GitHub. You need a free GitHub account.

What you write there is public. Never include names, document numbers, personal numbers (CNP), number plates, photos of documents or other personal details. If you need an example, use a made-up one, such as B-123-ABC.

Report a problem

In the same place, with “Ask a question”. So that it can be fixed, say:

  • what you did, step by step;
  • what happened, and what you expected to happen;
  • the app’s version, from Setări › Despre › Versiunea (you can copy it there);
  • your phone’s model and iOS version.

Security problems

If you found something that could expose someone’s data, don’t write about it in public. Report it privately, through GitHub’s private vulnerability reporting: only you and the people who look after the app see what you write.

Dosar cu Șină is free, and its code is public on GitHub.
