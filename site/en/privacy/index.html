Privacy policy

Effective 25 November 2026

Dosar cu Șină keeps a household’s documents, deadlines and bills. Everything you put in it stays with you: on your phone and, if you want, in your own iCloud.

In short

  • No account, no ads, no tracking and no analytics.
  • The app has no servers. Its developer receives nothing of your data, so it can’t be sold or given to anyone.
  • What you put in the binder stays on your phone and, when iCloud sync is on, in the private database of your own iCloud account.
  • Otherwise, a document leaves your phone only when you choose: when you send it to someone, make a backup, or read it with an AI service using your own key.

Where your data is kept

Sections, deadlines, documents, photos and notes are kept in the app’s own space on your phone, with the file protection iOS gives them.

When iCloud sync is on (Setări › iCloud și copii de siguranță), the binder is also kept in the private database of your iCloud account, so you have it on all your devices. Only you can reach it, with your account; the app’s developer can’t see it. Apple keeps it under iCloud’s terms. Sync is on from the start when the phone is signed into iCloud, and you turn it off in the same place. With sync off, everything stays on the phone only.

Secret codes, such as an electronic identity card’s PINs or an alarm code, are encrypted before they are saved. Their key is kept on the phone, where the iPhone keeps passwords (the Keychain), and a copy goes to iCloud Keychain if you use it, so the codes open on your other devices too.

What the app doesn’t do

  • It has no accounts and never asks for your name, email or phone number.
  • It has no servers and no central database.
  • It has no ads, analytics, tracking, or third-party components that send data.
  • It doesn’t sell or share your data with anyone.

How documents are read

When you photograph or add a document, the phone reads it by itself, offline: with iOS’s text recognition and, on phones that have it, Apple’s on-device language model. Nothing leaves the phone for this.

Reading with an AI service, if you want

You can have a document read better by an AI service: Google Gemini, OpenAI, Anthropic Claude, or a compatible service at an address you type. The app works fully without one.

  • The key is yours. You get it from the service you choose and add it in Setări › Citire cu AI. It stays on this phone only, in the Keychain, and goes only to the service you chose, with each request.
  • Only the document you choose is sent. When you tap “Citește mai bine cu …” (read better with …) on a document, a picture of its first page goes straight from your phone to that service. The service uses what it receives under its own terms. For Google Gemini, OpenAI and Anthropic Claude, the app shows you a link to them in Settings and before you send. A compatible service is your own choice, so the app has no link to its terms: it only reminds you to read them.
  • Nothing is sent by itself unless you turn on “Citește automat când nu sunt sigur” (read automatically when unsure). Even then, an identity document, or one from a protected category, is sent only after the app tells you and you tap “Trimite poza” (send the photo), every time.
  • A bank card is never sent, nor any page on which the phone sees a whole card number.
  • When you test your key or pick a model, the app only asks the service whether the key works and which models you can use. No document is sent.

Card numbers

From a bank card, the app keeps only the last four digits. A whole card number, typed by you or read off a page, is hidden before it is saved, wherever it is in the binder. A card’s photo is kept only if you ask for it.

Backups

A backup is a file you make yourself, whenever you want, and put wherever you choose: in Files, over AirDrop, in an email. You can protect it with a password; then the whole file is encrypted, and a forgotten password can’t be recovered. Secret codes are never written to a backup.

When you send a document

“Trimite” (send) sends only the document’s pages, without what the app read off them and without location or camera details. File names never contain your name or any number. They go only where you choose.

Face ID and locks

You can ask for Face ID or Touch ID (or the phone’s passcode) for the whole app or for some categories; Acte (documents) and Sănătate (health) start locked. Face ID is checked by the iPhone: the app only learns whether it succeeded, never anything about your face. What is locked never shows its details in search, widgets or notifications. With sync on, a category locked on one device is locked on all your devices; you open it only on the one in your hand, with Face ID.

Notifications, widgets and search

  • Notifications are local. The phone schedules them; they don’t come from a server. You turn them off in the app, in Setări › Notificări.
  • Widgets show what’s coming up. For a protected category they show general wording, with no details.
  • iPhone search (Spotlight) can find section names, deadlines and document titles. Never values, notes, text read off documents, or anything from a protected category. You turn it off in Setări › Protecție, with “Arată în căutarea iPhone-ului” (show in iPhone search).
  • Example data, from “Încarcă exemple” (load examples), never reaches notifications, widgets or search.

What the app asks for

Only what it uses, when it uses it:

  • The camera, to photograph documents.
  • Face ID, for locks.
  • Notifications, to remind you of deadlines.
  • The local network, only if you choose a compatible AI service on your home network, so the app can send it the document you choose.
  • Adding to Photos, only when you save the pages you send there. The app doesn’t read your photos: you choose what to add.

Calendar and contacts are used through the system’s own screens, without the app having access to them: you add only what you choose.

Reports Apple sends

If you chose, in your iPhone’s privacy settings, to share analytics with app developers, Apple may send the developer technical reports about the app’s crashes, under Apple’s terms. You can turn this off in the same place.

How to delete your data

  • In the app, you can delete any section, document or deadline at any time.
  • Deleting the app removes everything on the phone.
  • You delete the iCloud copy in your iPhone’s Settings, under iCloud, where you see the space each app uses.
  • Backups are your own files: delete them wherever you put them.

Children

The app isn’t made for children and collects no data from anyone, children included. A child’s documents you keep in the binder are kept like all the others: only with you.

Changes

If the app ever does anything else with data, this page changes first, with a new date at the top. Every version of it is public, in the page’s history on GitHub.

Questions

For questions, see Help. A security problem is reported privately, through GitHub, never in public.
