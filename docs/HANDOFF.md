# Handoff

A living document: where work stands, so the next person or agent can pick up. Keep "Current state" and "In progress" true. Append to "Last sessions" at the end of every session (newest first).

[SPEC.md](../SPEC.md) describes behaviour in full; this file is the short version plus what's moving.

## Known gaps

- **Loose "Factură …" deadlines from before.** Bills filed by earlier versions made a payment deadline under the home, not tied to a subscription. They stay as they are; nothing migrates them. New bills remind only through their subscription.
- **Supply address and direct debit, not tried on real bills.** The label tables use the usual wordings of the main providers; none was checked against a real bill or photo, and two-column layouts (billing address beside the supply address) rely on the label reader's columns. The supply address isn't kept on the subscription itself (only a home without an address takes it).

- **Personal documents, labels not checked on real papers.** None of the personal document templates has been tried on a real photo. The cazier's labels weren't checked against the official specimen (Hotărârea Guvernului nr. 345/2010, anexa 18). The national health card is not a gap: it stays typed in only, with no template, by the owner's decision, because its printed fields couldn't be confirmed against the official specimen (Ordinul CNAS nr. 520/2011). The green card follows the Council of Bureaux model (its English captions confirmed on a published national model) and BAAR's Romanian wordings from its own models, not from a Romanian green card; the validity dates printed in day, month and year boxes on one line ("01 11 2026 31 10 2027") aren't read as a range. The electronic signature has no official layout; its labels are the usual wordings of certificates. A name printed with no comma or address word before the town ("EXEMPLU ION BUCUREȘTI") keeps the town in the name; a firm without its legal form is taken for a person.
- **Card photos waiting.** A bank card shared from another app waits in the share inbox, and a captured one in capture's store, until it is filed or dismissed; only then do the pages go.

- **iCloud sync** to the person's private iCloud. The schema is ready; the store is local only.
- **Android port.** Paused; `android/` is the first version. The `.dosar` format is the contract.
- **Merging, what's left.** Every record merges by its change time (`ArchiveMerge`), but a merge never deletes: a record deleted on one phone comes back from an older backup (deletions need a record of their own, for the sync design). The rule is per record: two phones that change different fields of the same record keep only the later copy whole. Not tried with a real backup from one phone into another: a visit corrected on one phone, a page added to a document, a bill's PDF filed after its photo. Change times are written to the second in archives; two changes to one record in the same second on two phones keep the phone's own.
- **The share sheet's auto-close** is switched off, not removed (`ShareConfirmation.closesByItself`, `DS.Motion.confirmationDwell`), since the confirmation now stays until "Gata". Remove it once the owner has seen the new one on a phone and agrees.
- **The deadline page's inline reminder settings** are switched off, not removed (`ObligationPage.editsRemindersInline`), since the settings moved to `ReminderSettingsSheet`. Remove them once the owner has used the sheet and agrees.
- **Removing `ios/Legacy/`** (and `tests/legacy/`). Kept as a way back; remove only once the new app has been used for a while and the owner agrees.
- **Box order is kept on the device only.** It isn't part of the household data, so it won't sync or travel in an archive.

- **The legacy utility parser** (`RomanianUtilityParser`) is switched off, not removed: it sits in `ios/Legacy/Services/OCR/` with the capture adapter it had (`LegacyUtilityInvoiceParser`), and its old tests in `tests/legacy/ios/RomanianUtilityParserSuite.swift`. Bills are read by the bill kind's label table (`BillReader`, `ContractReader`, `UtilityProviders`). To switch back: move the file to `ios/DosarCuSina/Services/OCR/`, register `LegacyUtilityInvoiceParser()` in `CaptureEngine.standard` in place of `UtilityInvoiceParser()`, and put back its three lines in `scripts/architecture-exceptions.txt` (date-format ×2, format-string, romanian-locale ×2), which needs the owner's OK. Remove it, with its tests and the `RomanianUtilityParser` calls in `ios/Legacy/Views/`, once bills from the main providers have been read well on a phone and the owner agrees.
- **Bills, not tried on real ones.** The label tables use the wordings the first reader knew and the usual ones; none was checked against a real bill or photo. A bill printed as a table (old and new index in columns) relies on the label reader's columns.
- **Readings from a bill, what's left.** The index is recorded on the subscription by the review and by "Am rezolvat" with a photo of the bill, both showing its line first. Not tried on a real bill or a phone: the review's line, the lower-index question over a bill, the hero's *Ultimul index* at the accessibility sizes. The home's page doesn't show its subscriptions' indexes, and there is no list of readings on a subscription (only the last). A replaced meter is answered with *Contor nou* (not tried on a phone). A bill with two registers (day and night) records the first new index only, marked "Verifică" by the reader. The previous index is still read only to check the new one (`BillReader.previousIndex`). Deleting the bill's document leaves its reading.
- **Filed again, what's left.** One rule knows a paper already in the binder (`PaperIdentity`): the same document, or another photo or PDF of the same bill (review, "Am rezolvat") or of a visit's invoice (the visit form) logs one entry, and a bill joined from the repair list (`SameBillTwiceStep`) keeps one index and one *Factura de plătit*. Visits filed before have no number (known by their day, total and firm); one logged twice by another photo is joined from the repair list (`SameVisitTwiceStep`). The same number is certain only from the same issuer; from a firm named on one paper only, or on another day, "E altă factură" is offered. Two bills both paid with "Gata" keep both payments when joined. Not seen on a phone: "Factura e deja în dosar" and "E altă factură" in the review, the visit form and the "Am rezolvat" sheet at the accessibility sizes, the visit form's "Vezi factura" (it closes the form, then opens the visit), a bill's PDF filed after its photo, then its Istoric, its trend and its page's documents (the old copy archived); the repair list's two wordings.
- **Questions first, what's left.** Every question is asked before a unit writes (`StepQuestion`), and a test run stops on one asked too late (the app only logs it), but only on the paths tests take. A unit that fails for another reason fetches again what it touched: without it, reading a section's deadlines after a failed visit stopped the test run on the simulator; with it, a failed unit and a failed save are pinned readable, not every model and relationship one by one.
- **Values from a newer version, what's left.** Every object stored as JSON keeps what a newer build wrote inside it (`KeptObject`, `KeptList`, one rule: `JSONValue.laying`), in the database and through backups. Still not kept: a key a newer build adds to a deadline's own reminder profile in a backup (`reminders` on an obligation), since the database keeps that profile as plain columns and keeping it needs a database change; and what the phone keeps only for itself through "Înlocuiește tot" (its secret rows and sealed values) is carried as this build reads it, so a key a newer build wrote inside one of them, and a secret row this build can't read, are lost by a restore. A built-in category's old field keys renamed by the catalog (`TemplateStore`'s retired keys) move only what this build reads of their values.
- **Kind profiles, what's left.** Every kind's labels are its profile's table (`CaptureKindProfile.labels`, `commonLabels`, a policy's number and holder rows, a contract's codes), the shared readers' words `CaptureLexicon`'s, and the `labels-outside-their-table` check keeps lists out of reading code (any list named …Labels, …Words or …Prefixes outside the table files). An invoice's and the CIV's number labels are still `CompositeLabels.invoice` and `vehicleCard`, shared by several kinds, not rows of each kind's table. Which fields a kind keeps beyond what it is asked is still the families in `CaptureFieldRules` (a person's data, a policy's holder, a subscription's address), gated by the profile. Still written as patterns in the shared readers, not as rows: the talon's numbered fields (A, B, D.1, D.3, read by `registrationFields`) and the tax ID's labels (CUI, CIF, cod fiscal, as one pattern with optional dots). Only bills and identity documents are read as a layout (`LabelledLayout`, values in their columns); the other kinds' rows are read line by line, as before, until they are tried on real photos (see "Every kind's labels are its profile's table" in DECISIONS).
- **The on-device model, not tried on a phone.** It asks the kind's profile through a generation schema built for each kind (`OnDeviceSchema`), and the kind first while the phone is unsure. Tests pin the schema through Foundation Models' own encoding and map hand-made answers; the model itself never runs in tests or on GitHub. Each question has its own session and a budget from the model's context (`OnDevicePromptBudget`, pinned by tests); the system's token count is used from iOS 26.4, an estimate before. Not seen on a device with Apple Intelligence: whether its answers are as good as with the old fixed list, how long the two questions take on a long bill, and how much text the budget leaves on a real phone (the estimate is meant to be cautious). A failure of either question is the usual calm notice, and the phone's reading stands.
- **Category behaviour, what's left.** No screen names a built-in any more. Services (examples, the first-version converter, readiness trips, household rules) still name built-in keys, which the check allows. A category of one's own can't choose its visit wording or a place in the add menu's shortlist from the form; only an archive can carry them.
- **Filed papers from before version 3** have no `validUntil`; they show their deadline's day, as before, until refiled.
- **"Verifică" on a home's policy facts** is the repair list's entry, not a mark on the fields: a value has no stored "check this" flag yet.
- **Undo, what's left.** Every step a person takes from a screen returns a receipt and shows "Anulează" (SPEC 4.1a), deletions included: a firm, a category of one's own, the examples, a visit through capture or "Scriu eu" (a shared invoice waits in De verificat for the toast) and onboarding's first photo (on onboarding's own toast) since `claude/same-bill-and-undo`. Still without it: deleting from Istoric (decided: not undo) and reminder settings changed row by row. Deleting a firm, a category or the examples still asks first; whether it should now is the owner's call. Archiving a deadline is undoable but has no button. A visit undone after its new photo was filed can't be saved again with that photo: capture let go of the pages when the visit was kept. A receipt keeps what a delete took in memory while its toast shows (a section's pictures too); not measured on a large section. Not seen on a phone: the shell's toast above the tab bar (its height is measured, `measuringToastInset`), the deadline page's two new rows. Waiting for the sync design: whether an undone step should give its records a fresh change time (today it puts the old ones back, so a backup made between the step and its undo can win over the undo on a merge).

- **Card numbers, what's left.** Every save masks card numbers in every record's words (`FreeText`), and numbers an earlier version kept are offered in *Date de verificat* (`CardNumbersStep`). Not seen on a phone: the field's note ("Din numărul cardului păstrez doar ultimele patru cifre.") at the accessibility sizes and with VoiceOver, and the repair list's new entry. The repair reads every record's words at each launch, as the other steps read theirs; not measured on a large binder. A finding the owner leaves as it is ("Lasă așa") stays only until that record is saved again. ## How to pick up work

1. Read [AGENTS.md](../AGENTS.md), [docs/DECISIONS.md](DECISIONS.md) and the last few entries below.
2. Pick something from "Known gaps", an open issue, or what the owner asked for. Check "In progress" so you don't overlap.
3. If it changes what a person sees or a decision, propose it first (issue with the **Proposal** template, or a draft pull request).
4. Branch as `<person>/<topic>`, work in small conventional commits, run the relevant tests as you go and the whole suite once at the end.
5. Open a pull request with the template. Update this file in the same pull request.
