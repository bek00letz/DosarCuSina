## Known gaps

- **Loose "Factură …" deadlines from before.** Bills filed by earlier versions made a payment deadline under the home, not tied to a subscription. They stay as they are; nothing migrates them. New bills remind only through their subscription.
- **Supply address and direct debit, not tried on real bills.** The label tables use the usual wordings of the main providers; none was checked against a real bill or photo, and two-column layouts (billing address beside the supply address) rely on the label reader's columns. The supply address isn't kept on the subscription itself (only a home without an address takes it), and the on-device model isn't asked for it.

- **Personal documents capture doesn't read yet.** A green card, an electronic signature and the national health card have no template: a photo of one taken from a person is filed as one of their documents, and the person picks the type in Editează. The cazier's labels weren't checked against the official specimen (Hotărârea Guvernului nr. 345/2010, anexa 18), and none of the new templates has been tried on a real photo.
- **Card photos waiting.** A bank card shared from another app waits in the share inbox, and a captured one in capture's store, until it is filed or dismissed; only then do the pages go.

- **iCloud sync** to the person's private iCloud. The schema is ready; the store is local only.
- **Android port.** Paused; `android/` is the first version. The `.dosar` format is the contract.
- **One merge rule for every record type.** History entries, line items, readings and documents are added only when new; they have no `updatedAt` to compare. A round-trip test over every stored property is still to write.
- **The deadline page's inline reminder settings** are switched off, not removed (`ObligationPage.editsRemindersInline`), since the settings moved to `ReminderSettingsSheet`. Remove them once the owner has used the sheet and agrees.
- **Removing `ios/Legacy/`** (and `tests/legacy/`). Kept as a way back; remove only once the new app has been used for a while and the owner agrees.
- **The visit wording as a template field.** The visit flow's title and wording should come from the template, not from code.
- **Box order is kept on the device only.** It isn't part of the household data, so it won't sync or travel in an archive.
- **Architecture exceptions.** `scripts/architecture-exceptions.txt` lists the places that broke a rule before the check existed: built-in category keys in views, small tap targets, saves outside `UnitOfWork`, the system's error text, silent `try?` on actions and a few more. Each group says which piece of work removes it; remove lines as they're fixed.

- **The legacy utility parser** (`RomanianUtilityParser`) reads numbers through `RomanianNumber` but returns them as `Double`, so a meter index that could read two ways isn't marked "Verifică" when it comes through that parser. It goes when the parser becomes label tables (kind profiles).
- **Readings from a bill.** A meter index read off a bill isn't recorded as a reading on the home or subscription yet; only a section's hero value (the kilometres) is.
- **Values from a newer version, the rest.** Unknown field values, consequences and kinds are kept and read loudest. An unknown recurrence still reads as one-off (it is stored unchanged until edited), and archives carry the downgraded form; template sections and custom facts still fall back whole when one entry can't be read.
- **Kind profiles, what's left.** The on-device model still asks its own hand-written list of facts (`GeneratedDocumentFacts`) instead of the kind's `expectedKeys`, which needs a dynamic generation schema; label tables per kind for invoices, ITP reports, vet records and policies (the analyzer's inline label lists) and the legacy utility parser as label tables are still to do. Capture keeps its own copies of the day, money and role types (`CaptureDay`, `CaptureMoney`, `CaptureFieldRole`).
- **Category behaviour, what's left.** The add-section menu's shortlist and wording (`AddSectionFlow`), the visit wording (`VisitDraft`) and one refresh query (`UpcomingRoot`) still name built-ins. Services (examples, the first-version converter, readiness trips) still name built-in keys, which the check allows.
- **Filed papers from before version 3** have no `validUntil`; they show their deadline's day, as before, until refiled.
- **"Verifică" on a home's policy facts** is the repair list's entry, not a mark on the fields: a value has no stored "check this" flag yet.
- **Undo, what's left.** Every undoable action returns a receipt (SPEC 4.1a). Not undoable from a toast yet: pause, resume, archive and delete of a deadline, deleting a section or a document, and the review's "Salvează" (its receipt exists in `CaptureFiler.Outcome`, but the review closes without a toast). A visit undone after its new photo was filed can't be saved again with that photo: capture let go of the pages when the visit was kept. Waiting for the sync design: whether an undone step should give its records a fresh change time.

- **Siri and Shortcuts with the app lock on** open the app instead of answering. Not tried on a device yet.

- **Fixed waits.** A few screens still wait a fixed time instead of for the event: the next sheet after one closes (`ShellRouter`, 450 ms), scrolling to a Settings section (250 ms), the permission refresh (2 s), removing a dismissed inbox item after its toast (11 s).
- **One image scaler.** Downscaling is written in `CaptureDocumentStore` and `CloudCaptureExtractor`, upscaling in `ImageScaling`.
- **Rows checked by measuring, not by eye.** `DSAdaptiveStack`, wrapping badges and chips, and the 44 pt targets are pinned by tests that measure the layout; nobody has looked at them in the simulator at the accessibility sizes yet.

## How to pick up work

1. Read [AGENTS.md](../AGENTS.md), [docs/DECISIONS.md](DECISIONS.md) and the last few entries below.
2. Pick something from "Known gaps", an open issue, or what the owner asked for. Check "In progress" so you don't overlap.
3. If it changes what a person sees or a decision, propose it first (issue with the **Proposal** template, or a draft pull request).
4. Branch as `<person>/<topic>`, work in small conventional commits, run the relevant tests as you go and the whole suite once at the end.
5. Open a pull request with the template. Update this file in the same pull request.
